# Knowledge Lifecycle

Status: Current non-governing guidance.

The wiki holds only portable knowledge that remains useful across repository
copies. Reach, lifetime, and authority/status are independent:

- **Reach** is repository-portable, per-clone, or user-local. Only the first
  belongs in committed wiki pages.
- **Lifetime** is durable or condition-bound. Condition-bound guidance must
  state a useful review or retirement trigger.
- **Authority/status** distinguishes governing authority, current guidance,
  evidence, deferred research, and unresolved working material.

Current guidance must say what it means and what evidence supports it. When a
condition changes, remove retired material from current navigation. Preserve it
under `wiki/sources/` only when it remains useful historical evidence; otherwise
Git history is sufficient. Historical and deferred pages must not be presented
as current guidance.

Governing documents are the repository's authority. This wiki does not replace,
override, or silently reinterpret them.

# Repository Foundation

Part of the [ALP Skills Book](../README.md). See the [wiki index](index.md)
for related current knowledge.

## Purpose

ALP Skills now provides the durable home that earlier skill work lacked: its
Book records governing intent, and its wiki records accepted wrap-up knowledge.
This foundation exists before the dispatcher correction so that the correction
can be designed, reviewed, and retained here rather than reconstructed from a
past session.

## Current authority

The Book and explicit user decisions govern forward repository behavior. An
exact 2026-07-21 baseline of the installed Codex skills, role profiles, and
Antigravity companion now lives under `codex/` and `antigravity/`; it is
operational evidence, not a replacement for governing intent. The TG design
chapters remain current external evidence. The old Antigravity repository and
its seed wiki are historical evidence only until a recorded reconciliation
promotes a specific claim.

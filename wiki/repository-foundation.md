## Accepted delegation-economics policy

Routing optimizes weighted credit consumption and protects expensive root
Codex context, not raw token count. The dated July 2026 GPT-5.6 Sol:Terra:Luna
relative heuristic is approximately `5:2.5:1` within corresponding token
categories; it is a routing prior rather than a bill prediction and must be
revalidated when pricing or models change. Luna is preferred for bounded,
broad, multi-file, noisy, or repeatable implementation and validation work;
direct Sol has only a narrow conditional safe harbor. Work is sequential by
default, with no swarm or worker-count objective.

The sole recursion experiment is root-authorized Terra to exactly one fresh,
sequential, read-only Luna evidence child. Terra retains semantic judgment,
discloses child use, reconciles compact evidence, and has no retry, fallback,
or further-recursion authority. Whether this is net-positive remains an
empirical daily-use question, not a proven savings claim.

For manual EC workflow, Sol prepares and mechanically lints the EC directly;
Sol and the user review the Antigravity plan directly; and one fresh Terra
semantic review occurs after implementation evidence exists. There is no
delegated EC semantic preflight or separate Terra plan review.

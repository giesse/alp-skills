## Official source reviewed

[GitHub Spec Kit](https://github.github.com/spec-kit/) describes itself as an
extensible, intent-driven toolkit for specification-driven development. Its
default process is specification, plan, tasks, and implementation, with
Markdown artifacts feeding the next phase. Its
[philosophy](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/main/spec-driven.md)
places specifications ahead of code as the durable definition of what is built.

## Relevance to ALP Skills

The useful candidates to evaluate are its artifact flow, project-principles
input, reusable templates, and cross-artifact analysis. None is adopted here.
ALP's readable Book, its distinction from ordinary specification-driven work,
and the existing ownership of phases and acceptance remain the current model
unless a later governing decision changes them.
